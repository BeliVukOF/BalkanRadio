Privacy Policy

Balkan Radio for Android · Effective 21 October 2026 · Contact: BeliVukOF@users.noreply.github.com

English

Balkan Radio does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no tracking, no analytics and no advertising.

Your favorites, enabled regions, theme and language are saved only on this device.

Station lists and audio streams are downloaded directly from third-party servers. As with any internet request, those servers can see your IP address.

The app is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect data from children, because it collects no data at all.

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new effective date. Questions: BeliVukOF@users.noreply.github.com.
